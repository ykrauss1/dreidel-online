Yiddish narrator clips (yi_*.mp3)
  Synthesized with REYD — Reading Electronic Yiddish Documents (Webber, Lo, Bleaman), a Yiddish text-to-speech
  system trained on recordings of native Yiddish speakers (female voice, Lithuanian Yiddish), via the project's
  public demo. Pitch raised for a child-like voice.
