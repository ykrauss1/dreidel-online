Yiddish narrator clips (yi_*.mp3)
  Synthesized with REYD — Reading Electronic Yiddish Documents (Webber, Lo, Bleaman), a Yiddish text-to-speech
  system trained on recordings of native Yiddish speakers (female and male voices, Lithuanian Yiddish — 'bobe' and 'zeyde'), via the project's
  public demo. Each line is assembled from separately synthesized parts, with intonation added by hand (joy / sorrow /
  exclamation) and pauses at the commas. The female voice is pitched up a little.
  https://github.com/REYD-TTS
